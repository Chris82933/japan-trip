Vehicle

4WD van/SUV, not a true camper — you're staying in real beds each night. Get quotes from Peak Niseko Car Rental, plus a couple of local winter-van outfits to compare.

Quiet powder day

Day 4 is Niseko Annupuri — the calmest Niseko United sector, lift-served with great trees. Prefer a snowcat day instead? Chisenupuri (Hokkaido Backcountry Club) is the affordable, unguided swap — weather-dependent, so keep it flexible.

Lift tickets

Kiroro, Moiwa, and Rusutsu each run separate passes from Niseko United — buy day-by-day rather than a single multi-day pass.
